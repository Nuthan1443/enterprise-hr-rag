ENTERPRISE HR POLICY DOCUMENT
Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: January 2025
================================================================

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
New joiners receive leave on a pro-rata basis from the date of joining.
Unused leave up to 12 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year.
Leave encashment is permitted for a maximum of 6 days per year during appraisal cycle.

1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per year.
Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

1.3 Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity leave: 26 weeks for the first two children, 12 weeks thereafter.
Paternity leave: 5 working days, to be availed within 6 months of childbirth.
Adoption leave: 12 weeks for the primary caregiver.

1.4 Emergency / Bereavement Leave
Up to 5 days of paid emergency leave for immediate family bereavement.
Immediate family includes spouse, children, parents, and siblings.

SECTION 2: PERFORMANCE APPRAISAL POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Appraisal Cycle
The company follows an annual appraisal cycle from April to March.
Mid-year check-ins are conducted in October for all employees.
Performance ratings are: Outstanding (5), Exceeds Expectations (4),
Meets Expectations (3), Needs Improvement (2), Unsatisfactory (1).

2.2 Increment Policy
Increment percentages are linked to performance ratings:
- Outstanding: 20–30%
- Exceeds Expectations: 15–20%
- Meets Expectations: 8–12%
- Needs Improvement: 0–5%
- Unsatisfactory: No increment, placed on PIP

2.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees rated Unsatisfactory are placed on a 90-day PIP.
PIP goals are set jointly by the manager and HR.
Failure to meet PIP targets may result in termination.

SECTION 3: REMOTE WORK & HYBRID POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Hybrid Work Model
The company follows a 3-2 hybrid model: 3 days in office, 2 days remote.
Office days are Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday by default.
Team leads may adjust office days based on project requirements.

3.2 Remote Work Eligibility
Employees completing 6 months of tenure are eligible for hybrid work.
Employees on PIP are required to work from office full-time.
Remote work from outside the city requires prior manager approval.

3.3 Equipment Policy
The company provides a laptop and one external monitor for remote work.
Internet allowance of ₹1,000/month is provided for remote employees.
Employees are responsible for the security of company equipment at home.

================================================================

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT
----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Workplace Behavior
All employees must maintain professional conduct in office and virtual settings.
Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are strictly prohibited.
Violations must be reported to HR within 48 hours of occurrence.

4.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal business interests that may conflict with company interests.
Moonlighting (working for a competitor) without written approval is a terminable offense.
Gifts above ₹2,000 in value from vendors must be disclosed and returned.

4.3 Data Confidentiality
Employees must not share proprietary company data with external parties.
All confidential documents must be stored in company-approved cloud systems.
Violation of data confidentiality may result in immediate termination and legal action.

================================================================

SECTION 5: COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Salary Structure
CTC comprises: Basic (40%), HRA (20%), Special Allowance (30%), PF Contribution (10%).
Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.

5.2 Health Insurance
The company provides group health insurance of ₹5 lakhs per employee.
Family floater option covers spouse and up to 2 children at ₹3 lakhs additional premium borne by the employee.
Pre-existing conditions are covered after a 2-year waiting period.

5.3 Provident Fund
PF is deducted at 12% of basic salary from both employee and employer.
Employees can withdraw PF after 5 years of continuous service for tax-free benefits.

5.4 Gratuity
Gratuity is payable after 5 years of continuous service.
Formula: (Last drawn basic salary × 15 × years of service) / 26

================================================================

SECTION 6: RECRUITMENT & ONBOARDING
----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Referral Policy
Employee referral bonus: ₹25,000 for non-tech roles, ₹50,000 for tech roles.
Referral bonus is paid in two installments: 50% at joining, 50% after 6 months.
Referrals from direct reporting managers are not eligible.

6.2 Onboarding
New joiners attend a 3-day mandatory induction program.
IT access and equipment are provisioned within 24 hours of joining.
A buddy is assigned to every new joiner for the first 60 days.

6.3 Background Verification
All offers are subject to successful background verification.
BGV covers employment history (7 years), education, and criminal record.
Offers may be revoked if discrepancies are found post-joining.

================================================================

SECTION 7: SEPARATION POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Notice Period
Notice period is 90 days for all employees with tenure above 1 year.
Notice period is 30 days for employees in probation (first 6 months).
Notice period buyout is allowed at the discretion of the business unit head.

7.2 Exit Process
Employees must complete a structured exit checklist including knowledge transfer.
Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of last working day.
Experience letters and relieving letters are issued within 30 days.

7.3 Rehire Policy
Former employees are eligible for rehire after a gap of 6 months.
Employees terminated for misconduct or data breach are permanently ineligible for rehire.
